Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Almana Recovery & Aesthetics, LLC (“Almana,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website at almanarecovery.com and to the services and communications we provide.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information needed to deliver your care, including:

  • Contact information — your name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Appointment and service details — the services you request, your scheduling preferences, and information about your procedure or recovery that you choose to share so we can care for you safely.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications — messages you send us by phone, text (SMS), WhatsApp, email, or our website contact form.
  • Website usage — basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and secure.

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, coordinate, and provide your recovery and aesthetic care.
  • To communicate with you about appointments, care instructions, and questions — including by phone, SMS, and WhatsApp where you have opted in.
  • To process payments and manage billing.
  • To coordinate, at your direction, with your surgical or medical team.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our services, and to comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

  • Customer data is not shared with 3rd parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
  • Mobile opt-in and consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose. Any information sharing that may be mentioned elsewhere in this policy excludes mobile opt-in data.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate — for example, our scheduling platform, payment processor, and messaging provider — only as needed to deliver our services and under obligations of confidentiality. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our clients, staff, or others. We do not sell your personal information.

Text Messaging (SMS) & WhatsApp

With your consent, we send you text messages related to your care, such as scheduling, confirmations, care instructions, and answers to your questions. Message frequency varies based on your communication needs. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Your mobile opt-in and consent are never shared with third parties, as stated above. Full messaging terms are available in our Terms & Conditions.

Health & Personal Information

Any health-related details you share with us are treated as private and confidential and are used only to provide your care. Almana is a private, self-pay concierge service and does not bill insurance. We limit the personal and health information we collect to what is necessary to serve you.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information protected and to limit access to those who need it to serve you.

Your Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
  • You may opt out of text messages at any time (reply STOP) and of emails using the unsubscribe option where provided.

Children’s Privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent version, and changes take effect when posted on this page.
