Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are your nurses registered nurses (RNs)?

Yes. Almana was founded and is run by two registered nurses, Analia Leites, RN and Alma Hernandez-Hercules, RN, licensed in Virginia, Maryland, and the District of Columbia. Your care is provided or directly supervised by a registered nurse.

Which procedures do you support?

We care for patients recovering from cosmetic plastic surgery, including abdominoplasty (tummy tuck), liposuction and body contouring, Brazilian butt lift, breast augmentation, lift or reduction, facelift, and rhinoplasty, as well as other outpatient surgeries where your surgeon recommends skilled recovery support.

Where do you provide care?

At your home or hotel across Northern Virginia, Washington DC, and Southern Maryland. Lymphatic massage and wound care are also available in our office at 6559 Edsall Road, Springfield, Virginia, by appointment.

Do you work with my surgeon?

Yes. We follow your surgeon's written post-operative protocol exactly and coordinate with their office on drains, sutures, medications, and any concern that comes up during recovery.

What does overnight care include?

A registered nurse stays with you through the night to monitor vital signs, manage pain and medication, assist with mobility, bathroom visits, and compression garments, and respond to anything that arises. Before nursing care ends, your nurse provides thorough hands-on teaching to whoever is continuing your care and support at home.

Do you remove drains, staples, and stitches?

Yes. Drain, staple, and suture removal are performed by a registered nurse at the time your surgeon specifies, at home or in our office.

What are Companion Services?

Non-medical support for the stretch after nursing care ends but before you are fully back on your feet. A companion picks up medications, helps with mobility around the home, drives you to follow-up appointments, and helps with everyday tasks such as light meals and errands. Companions are not nurses; if a clinical need comes up, a registered nurse steps back in.

Do you offer transportation from the surgical center?

Yes. Private, discreet transportation from the surgical center to your home or hotel, with a nurse accompanying you.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Pre-operative recovery consultations are free. Call or text (703) 755-8045 or send the form below.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Our founders are bilingual. Hablamos español. Visite nuestro sitio en español.

How do I pay?
